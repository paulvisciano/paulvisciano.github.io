## News (reputable sources only, last ~48 hours)

1. **US FTC considers requiring disclosure of personalized pricing**  
   Reuters, 19 Aug 2026  
   https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-ftc-considers-requiring-disclosure-personalized-pricing-2026-08-19/  
   FTC released a draft enforcement policy: undisclosed use of personal data (browsing history, location, shopping habits) to set individualized prices likely violates the FTC Act's ban on deceptive practices.
